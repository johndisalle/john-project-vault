Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app > Settings (gear icon) > Subscription section > "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your device's Settings > tap your name > Subscriptions > Project+ Vault > Cancel Subscription. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

Is this app affiliated with CompTIA?

No. Project+ Vault is an independent study tool. All questions are original and created for educational purposes. "CompTIA" and "Project+" are trademarks of CompTIA, Inc.

How many questions are included?

The app includes 200+ original practice questions covering all 4 PK0-005 exam domains, plus 4 full-length 90-question mock exams.

What's the difference between Free and Premium?

Free: 50 questions per day with basic practice quizzes.
Premium: Unlimited questions, all mock exams, flashcards, detailed analytics, and ad-free experience. Available as $4.99/month or $19.99 lifetime.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings (gear icon) > scroll to "Danger Zone" > "Reset All Progress". Warning: this permanently deletes all data.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes! All questions and features work offline. An internet connection is only needed for purchases and restore.

What iOS version is required?

Project+ Vault requires iOS 18.0 or later. It works on iPhone and iPad.
