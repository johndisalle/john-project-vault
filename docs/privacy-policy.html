Project+ Vault

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 3, 2026

Overview

Project+ Vault ("the App") is developed and operated by Ellasid. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Project+ Vault is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Quiz Progress & Scores: Stored locally on your device using UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.
  • Purchase History: Managed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not store or process payment information.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • No location data
  • No analytics or tracking data
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No third-party analytics SDKs
  • No data shared with third parties

Local Storage

All your study progress, quiz results, bookmarks, and mastery data are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, this data is permanently removed.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive transaction verification data through StoreKit 2 to unlock premium features. We do not have access to your payment details.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The App is an educational tool suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
